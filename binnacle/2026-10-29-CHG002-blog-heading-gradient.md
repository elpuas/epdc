# Blog heading gradient

## What changed

- Applied the existing brand gradient to headings in individual blog posts across all languages, including headings containing emphasized text.
- Scoped the change to blog posts; the shared Labs layout is unaffected.

## Why

To keep article headings consistent with the site's default title styling.

## Files affected

- `src/layouts/BlogPost.astro`
- `src/styles/global.css`

## Breaking changes

None expected.
