# 2027 Homepage Neural Journey Refresh

## What changed

- Replaced the homepage rotating-verb hero with localized commercial messaging in English, Spanish, and Italian.
- The hero and the first services section now share one Three.js scene, renderer, and viewport-sized canvas. The hero's original foreground, midground, and background topologies seed curved branches that grow into four independently configured service formations as the visitor scrolls. A shared formation profile defines topology, parent branch, depth, pulse, drift, and signals so later sections can extend the graph.
- Service areas use asymmetric placement and normal accessible DOM text. Each formation develops before its title and copy reveal; upward scrolling reverses that sequence. The renderer pauses when the wrapper is offscreen or the tab is hidden. Mobile uses fewer visible nodes and signals at a lower render resolution.
- Replaced broad service-area radial glows with independently seeded midground branches and sparse, partial background clusters. Foreground edges now branch through satellite hubs rather than converging on a single node; only active anchors retain localized micro-glow.
- Reduced-motion and unavailable-WebGL states show completed static SVG formations and visible text without loading Three.js. The following value statement remains an editorial conclusion.

## Why

The homepage now presents EPDC's practical combination of AI, automation, and custom digital development while keeping strategic judgment and technical quality central to the message. The hero-to-services passage reads as one connected neural system instead of two separate effects.

## Breaking changes

None. Existing homepage routes, metadata, lower sections, navigation, and content collections remain unchanged.
