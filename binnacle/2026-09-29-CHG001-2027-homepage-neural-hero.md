# 2026 Homepage Neural Hero Refresh

## What changed

- Replaced the homepage rotating-verb hero with localized commercial messaging in English, Spanish, and Italian.
- Added a decorative Three.js neural field with three distinct depth planes: active dendritic foreground clusters, a broader, clearly visible but lower-intensity midground with limited signals, and a sparse distant field behind the protected copy. The hero now uses the full available desktop width and a copy-protection gradient over the canvas; foreground and midground use separate, subtle pointer parallax. It loads only for desktop fine-pointer devices with WebGL and without reduced-motion preference.
- Added an intentional static CSS field for mobile, reduced-motion, and unsupported-WebGL experiences.

## Why

The homepage now presents EPDC's practical combination of AI, automation, and custom digital development while keeping strategic judgment and technical quality central to the message.

## Breaking changes

None. Existing homepage routes, metadata, lower sections, navigation, and content collections remain unchanged.
